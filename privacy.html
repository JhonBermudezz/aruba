Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This policy explains what happens to the details you share with One Happy Picnic Aruba when you use this website or book a picnic with us.

What we collect

The booking form asks for the experience, date, number of guests, your name, your email and, if you choose, your WhatsApp number and notes about your occasion.

The form does not store anything on our servers. When you press send, your browser prepares a message that you send to us yourself on WhatsApp or Instagram. We only receive what you send.

How we use it

  • To check availability and reply to your request.
  • To plan your picnic, including food preferences and allergies you tell us about.
  • To send you the location of your setup and any updates on the day.

We never sell your details or use them for advertising lists.

Photos

We sometimes share photos of our setups on Instagram. We only post photos that show you or your guests when you say yes first.

Third party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Messages you send us go through WhatsApp or Instagram, which have their own privacy policies. This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Your choices

Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the details you have shared. Send us a message on Instagram at @onehappypicnic.aruba and we will take care of it within 30 days.
