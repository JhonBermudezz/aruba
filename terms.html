Booking terms

Last updated October 14, 2026

These terms apply to every picnic, proposal setup and charcuterie order with One Happy Picnic Aruba.

Your booking

  • A request sent through this website is not a confirmed booking. Your date is confirmed once we reply and you complete payment.
  • Picnics last two hours unless we agree on extra time.
  • We send you a map pin of your setup before the picnic. Please arrive on time, as the end time does not move for late arrivals.

Cancellations

  • Cancel up to 24 hours before your picnic for a full refund.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours of the picnic, no shows and late arrivals are not refunded.

Weather

If rain or strong wind makes your picnic unsafe or impossible, we offer a new date or a full refund. Short showers that pass before your start time are not a reason to cancel on either side.

Food and allergies

Tell us about allergies and dietary needs when you book. We do our best to adapt the menu, but our kitchen handles nuts, dairy and gluten.

Care of the setup

Decor, tableware and textiles belong to One Happy Picnic Aruba. Items that are damaged or missing at the end of a picnic may be charged at replacement cost.

Beach rules

We hold the beach permit for your setup. Please follow local beach rules, keep glass on the table and leave the sand as beautiful as you found it.
