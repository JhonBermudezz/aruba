Good to know before you book

Still have a question? Message us on Instagram or WhatsApp and we will answer the same day.

How long does a picnic last?

Every picnic is two hours on the sand. Want to stay longer? Ask for extra time when you book.

Which beaches do you set up on?

Eagle Beach and Surfside Beach are our favorites, and we can set up at other beaches on request. Before your picnic we send you a map pin of your exact spot.

How much does a picnic cost?

Sunset picnics start at $99 per person and include the setup, food, drinks, beach permit and cleanup. Proposals, large groups and custom themes are quoted on WhatsApp.

What food and drinks are included?

A shared spread with our charcuterie, fresh fruit, snacks and sweets, plus soft drinks, a water station and a welcome bottle of Prosecco or wine.

Vegetarian, gluten free or allergies? Tell us when you book and we adapt the menu.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. A deposit through a secure payment link holds your date and time on our calendar. We confirm the amount and when the balance is due on WhatsApp.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel up to 24 hours before your picnic for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours and no shows are not refunded.

What happens if the weather turns?

Aruba sits outside the hurricane belt and showers are usually short. If the weather makes your picnic impossible, we move it to another date or refund you.

Can you help me plan a surprise proposal?

Yes. We plan the timing with you on WhatsApp, keep the setup a secret until you arrive and can book a photographer to capture the moment.

How far in advance should I book?

A few days ahead is usually enough. For proposals, groups and holiday weeks, book two weeks ahead. Last minute? Message us, we often have openings.

Are kids welcome?

Of course. Families are welcome and we can adjust the menu for little ones.

Do you deliver charcuterie boards?

Yes. Boards can be picked up or delivered to your hotel, villa or boat. Order at least a day ahead.
